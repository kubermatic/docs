How the building blocks of KDP work under the hood:

- **Workspaces**: organisations and projects, and how they keep tenants apart.
- **Services**: how providers offer APIs and consumers enable and use them.
- **The API Sync Agent**: how service clusters connect to KDP.
- **Blueprints**: services composed from other services.
- **RBAC**: permissions in and across workspaces.
