Task-oriented guides, grouped by who does the task:

- **Platform Administration**: run and upgrade KDP itself.
- **Service Providers**: publish services into the KDP catalog, compose them and shape how the
  dashboard renders them.
- **Platform Users**: organize your work, give people and machines access, and consume services.
